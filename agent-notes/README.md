owner. Verify observations against current code; maintain notes when their evidence
or assumptions change. Documentation policy lives in [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

- [ddb notes](../packages/ddb/agent-notes/README.md) — Collection, archive operations, Daycap mapping, and shared record semantics.
- [Web notes](../packages/web/agent-notes/README.md) — Static consumer builds, timeline presentation, and shared-chunk playback.
- [Publish notes](../packages/publish/agent-notes/README.md) — Versioned day JSON, shared timelapse assets, R2 upload, and portable consumers.
- [AutoRedact notes](../packages/autoredact/agent-notes/README.md) — Scanning, review, coverage, and decision manifests.
